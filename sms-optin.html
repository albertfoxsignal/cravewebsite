Opting In

Crave Robotics only sends text messages to customers who ask for them. You opt in either by using the sign-up form above, or by texting a keyword shown on the signage at a CraveBot vending machine — for example, texting a promo keyword to claim a discount, or texting "Support" to reach our team. We never auto-enroll phone numbers, purchase contact lists, or send unsolicited messages. If you never text us, you'll never hear from us.

Opting Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time and you'll be unsubscribed immediately — one confirmation message will follow, then nothing further. Reply HELP for assistance, or reach us at help@craverobotics.co.
